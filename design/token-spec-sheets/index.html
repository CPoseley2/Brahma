Brave Shots

Yellow

Canonical iconography

Use the upper target-and-ball front with the upper single-rooster reverse as the canonical pair. The lower pair remains an approved alternate.

Player promise

I tried something bold, even when it might not work.

Coach looks for

Taking a shot, trying the other foot, facing a defender, recovering from a miss, or trying again.

Visual language

  • Target, ball flight, shot trail, and forward motion
  • Yellow field with decisive black typography
  • Celebrate the attempt—not only the result
  • Avoid bullseye language that makes accuracy the award

Coach Portal quick start

Welcome to the coaching team.

Your account now includes the Coaching Dashboard while preserving your Parent view and access to your child’s season story.

Open the Coach Portal

Start here

  1. Open Dashboard for the next practice, team status, and recent notes.
  2. Use Season Plan and Drill Cards to prepare a session.
  3. Launch Field Mode from a practice when you are on the field.
  4. Use Messages for team announcements and private guardian replies.

Player information is private

Roster contacts, attendance, development notes, and private conversations are for team operations only. Share family-facing celebrations deliberately and keep private coaching notes inside the portal.

Switching views

Use the Workspace menu in the top bar to move between Coach and Parent. Your Parent view remains scoped to your own child and family conversations.

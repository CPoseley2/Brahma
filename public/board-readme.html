What this demo is for

The app turns preseason coordination into visible, testable decisions. It uses synthetic data—100 teams, 1,200 players, 20 fields, and a 12-week season—so it should be treated as an operating model, not as final club financials.

Plan the whole season

Compare complete schedules, field capacity, weather contingencies, and readiness before publishing a trusted plan.

Make the budget legible

Model registration policy, coach-family waivers, and expense allocations in terms the Board can review.

Operate equipment responsibly

Record invoices, scale orders in 50-player increments, use live vendor links, and track kit handoff to coaches.

Communicate with confidence

Keep the published season stable and use targeted parent communication when a change actually needs an announcement.

Key policy insight in the demo

The Budget Policy Lab compares a flat registration price with late-fee alternatives and a coach-family waiver. Under the demo assumptions, the flat fee and one waived coach-family registration per team still fund the season. Late fees add revenue, but are not required in the modeled case.

Before adopting any policy, replace the synthetic inputs with confirmed enrollment, registration price, field contracts, vendor quotes, scholarship commitments, and invoices.

Recommended Board next steps

  1. Review the demo as an operations model.
  2. Confirm registration assumptions, enrollment forecast, vendor quotes, and coach-waiver policy.
  3. Authorize a limited preseason pilot using actual club data.
  4. Approve one published season plan and define when a weather contingency may replace it.
